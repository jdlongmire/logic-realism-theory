# Actualizability, bridge identity, and nonlocal coherence: reconciliation audit
Status: ANALYSIS / OPEN OBLIGATIONS
Date: 2026-11-08
Authority: companion to the November information ontology notes; no canonical amendment.

## Source-anchored finding
The current [prephysics conditions](../../theory/000-prephysics-conditions.md) expressly distinguishes admissibility from actuality (sections 5-6, including 'Description is not existence. Admissibility is not actuality'). It states that actualization is irreducible to logical admissibility. This **supports** the November hierarchy A ⊆ P ⊆ C.

The [core physics](../../theory/100-LRT-CORE-PHYSICS.md) gives both χ ⊢ AΩ (ontological grounding) and AΩ := L3(I∞) (constitutive specification, lines 41-57 at review). The latter is glossed as the *actualized domain* consisting of all L3-admissible configurations. Read literally as set equality, this conflicts with the possibility of coherent but physically unactualized configurations. The two statements cannot be treated as interchangeable without a semantic clarification.

The existing [entanglement paper](../../theory/104-LRT-ENTANGLEMENT.md) already develops nonseparability, shared actualization, Bell correlations, Tsirelson's bound, and no-signaling. NLC-1 should therefore be evaluated as a proposed clarification of that account, **not represented as the first LRT treatment of entanglement**. The paper's stated derivations require independent premise scrutiny.

The [entailment audit](../../05-work-packages/WP-LRT-ENT-0001-entailment-audit/entailment-matrix.md) already labels the bridge as ONTOLOGICAL / SURVIVES_CONDITIONALLY and identifies subsequent reconstruction steps requiring imported structure.

## Candidate reconciliations (none adopted)
**Option A: Restricted domain.** Define I∞ in the bridge as the domain of *selected* configurations, not all coherent conceptual configurations. Risk: this contradicts the description of I∞ as total possibility space; requires explicit restriction and motivation.

**Option B: Distinguish eligibility and selection.** Introduce C = {i in I∞ : L3(i)} and AΩ = Act(C), with Act a primitive actualization operation. This fits prephysics irreducibility and the November hierarchy but requires amending the literal bridge identity and checking every downstream dependency.

**Option C: Modal/ontological reinterpretation.** Retain AΩ := L3(I∞) as a constitutive characterization of the *kind* of structure actuality must have, while denying literal extensional equality. This preserves historical notation but requires formally explicit semantics and may weaken claimed entailment.

## Disposition
- Logical coherence is necessary for actualizability: **AXIOMATIC / ARGUED**.
- Coherence sufficient for physical actuality: **CONTESTED**, especially against prephysics sections 5-6.
- NLC-1 distance-invariance of consistency: **LOGICAL TRUISM / DEFINITIONAL**, unless an additional nontrivial constraint is specified.
- Entanglement from L3 alone: **NOT DERIVED**.
- CHSH quantum bound from Hilbert formalism: **ESTABLISHED MATHEMATICS**, not yet an independent LRT entailment.
- LRT-specific nonlocal-coherence mechanism: **CONJECTURAL**.
