abstract: |
  How does the classical world emerge from quantum mechanics under Logic Realism Theory? Standard
  decoherence theory explains the suppression of interference through environmental entanglement but
  does not resolve the measurement problem — it selects a preferred basis without explaining why one
  outcome obtains. LRT's binary actualization primitive provides the missing element: decoherence
  identifies which observables become actualization-ready, and A selects the determinate outcome.
  This paper develops the LRT account of classicality, einselection, and the quantum-to-classical
  transition.
