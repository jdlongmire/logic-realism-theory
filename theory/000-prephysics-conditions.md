# The Minimal Preconditions of Physical Reality

## 1. Statement of Aim

This section argues that physics, insofar as it describes a real and intelligible world, presupposes three irreducible conditions:

1. logical admissibility
2. informational structure
3. actualization

The claim is not that these replace physics. The claim is that they are prior conditions for physics to have a subject matter at all.

---

## 2. Definitions

**Definition 1. Physical state**
A physical state is a determinate, physically manifest condition, event, or configuration capable of entering into lawful relations and physical description.

**Definition 2. Logical admissibility**
A state is logically admissible iff it does not violate identity, non-contradiction, or excluded middle in the respect relevant to its determination.

**Definition 3. Informational structure**
Informational structure is the determinate content by virtue of which a state is distinguishable, describable, and lawfully related to other states.

**Definition 4. Actualization**
Actualization is the obtaining of a physical state as manifest reality rather than as merely possible or formally specifiable content.

**Definition 5. Dynamical formalism**
A dynamical formalism is a mathematical structure, such as a Lagrangian or Hamiltonian, that constrains or generates the lawful evolution of already-defined physical states.

---

## 3. First Thesis: Physical Reality Presupposes Logical Admissibility

**Proposition 1**
Every physical theory presupposes that actual physical states are logically admissible.

**Argument**
P1.1 Physics describes actual states, transitions, measurements, and relations only insofar as they are determinate.
P1.2 A determinate state must be identifiable as what it is.
P1.3 A state that is both itself and not itself in the same respect at the same time is not determinate.
P1.4 A contradictory state cannot function as an actual physical state without collapsing the distinction between what obtains and what does not obtain.
C1 Therefore, physical actuality presupposes logical admissibility.

**Commentary**
This claim is modest and strong. It does not assert that physics is reducible to logic. It asserts that physics cannot proceed without the ontological force of logical constraint. No measurement outcome is ever accepted as a physically instantiated contradiction. Logical inadmissibility is not treated as a rare physical case. It is treated as non-instantiable.

---

## 4. Second Thesis: Physical Reality Presupposes Informational Structure

**Proposition 2**
Every physical theory presupposes that physical states are informationally structured.

**Argument**
P2.1 Physics describes reality in terms of distinguishable states, observables, symmetries, conservation relations, transition amplitudes, and boundary conditions.
P2.2 Whatever is distinguishable and describable as a state has determinate content.
P2.3 Determinate state-content is informationally structured.
C2 Therefore, physics presupposes informational structure.

**Commentary**
The term "information" here is not limited to Shannon information. Nor does it presuppose a digital ontology. It names structured determinacy. If one prefers, "informational structure" may be replaced with "structured state-content" without loss. The point is simple: physics never encounters bare, contentless being. It always encounters states as differentiated, describable, and lawfully related.

---

## 5. Third Thesis: Logical Admissibility and Informational Structure Do Not Suffice for Manifestation

**Proposition 3**
Logical admissibility and informational structure are jointly necessary but not jointly sufficient for physical manifestation.

**Argument**
P3.1 A possible state need not be an actual state.
P3.2 A formally describable state-space does not entail that any member of that space is physically instantiated.
P3.3 Logical consistency alone does not produce manifestation.
P3.4 Informational content alone does not produce manifestation.
C3 Therefore, logical admissibility and informational structure do not suffice for physical manifestation.

**Commentary**
This is the hinge of the argument. One may specify a mathematically coherent possibility space, yet nothing follows from that alone concerning physical manifestation. Description is not existence. Admissibility is not actuality. Structure is not event. Something further is required if a world is to obtain rather than merely remain formally describable.

---

## 6. Fourth Thesis: Physical Manifestation Requires Actualization

**Proposition 4**
Physical manifestation requires a primitive of actualization.

**Argument**
P4.1 Physical manifestation occurs.
P4.2 If physical manifestation occurs, then something accounts for the obtaining of actual states rather than merely possible states.
P4.3 Neither logical admissibility nor informational structure alone accounts for the obtaining of actual states.
C4 Therefore, physical manifestation requires a further principle: actualization.

**Commentary**
"Actualization" is not a placeholder for ignorance. It names the irreducible difference between the possible and the actual. A formalism may enumerate possibilities. It may constrain transitions among admissible states. It does not thereby explain why any state is physically manifest at all. The principle of actualization marks that ontological difference.

---

## 7. Irreducibility of the Three Conditions

**Proposition 5**
Logical admissibility, informational structure, and actualization are irreducible with respect to one another.

**Argument**
P5.1 If logical admissibility is reduced to informational structure, then the admissibility conditions governing information must already be presupposed.
P5.2 If informational structure is reduced to logical admissibility, then admissibility remains without any determinate content to be admitted.
P5.3 If actualization is reduced to either admissibility or informational structure, then possibility is collapsed into manifestation without argument.
C5 Therefore, logical admissibility, informational structure, and actualization are irreducible.

**Commentary**
Each term does distinct work.

Logical admissibility answers: what cannot be.
Informational structure answers: what is available as determinate possible content.
Actualization answers: what obtains.

If any one is removed, the explanatory frame collapses.

---

## 8. Downstream Character of Dynamical Formalisms

**Proposition 6**
Lagrangian and Hamiltonian formalisms are downstream constraints on evolution within an already logically actualized dynamical order.

**Argument**
P6.1 A Lagrangian presupposes a defined state space, variables, and admissible relations.
P6.2 A Hamiltonian presupposes canonical variables, a phase-space structure, and an already-defined dynamical system.
P6.3 What presupposes an already-defined and actualized dynamical order is not ontologically primitive with respect to that order.
C6 Therefore, Lagrangian and Hamiltonian formalisms are downstream rather than primitive.

**Commentary**
This point matters for category order. The Lagrangian and Hamiltonian do real explanatory work within physics. They constrain the lawful unfolding of already manifest systems. They do not explain why there is a non-contradictory, determinate, physically manifest order for them to govern. They formalize manifested action. They do not ground actualization itself.

---

## 9. Final Theorem

**Theorem**
The minimal preconditions of physical reality are logical admissibility, informational structure, and actualization.

**Proof**
From Proposition 1, physical reality presupposes logical admissibility.
From Proposition 2, physical reality presupposes informational structure.
From Proposition 4, physical reality presupposes actualization.
From Proposition 5, these three are irreducible.
From Proposition 6, standard dynamical formalisms are downstream of these preconditions.
Therefore, the minimal preconditions of physical reality are logical admissibility, informational structure, and actualization. $\blacksquare$

---

## 10. Corollaries

**Corollary 1**
Physics is not self-grounding.

Physics describes the lawful behavior of manifested systems, but it presupposes conditions that its own formalism does not generate from within itself.

**Corollary 2**
Logical laws are ontologically constraining, not merely psychologically useful.

The laws of logic do not merely regulate human thought about reality. They constrain what can be physically actualized.

**Corollary 3**
Information is prior to physical description in the sense of structured determinacy.

Physics can only describe a world that is already differentiated into determinate, lawfully related content.

**Corollary 4**
Actualization cannot be replaced by formal possibility.

No enumeration of possible states explains why any state becomes manifest reality.

---

## 11. Objection Handling

**Objection 1: "This is trivial."**
Reply:
Foundational claims are often trivial in the sense of being constantly used. Their importance lies in their priority, not their obscurity. The issue is not novelty of wording. The issue is whether physics can proceed without these commitments. It cannot.

**Objection 2: "Information is vague."**
Reply:
Replace "information" with "structured determinacy of possible and actual states." The argument remains unchanged.

**Objection 3: "Actualization is empty."**
Reply:
Only if one assumes that formal specification entails physical instantiation. That assumption is precisely what is in dispute and has not been established.

**Objection 4: "Quantum theory undermines classical logic."**
Reply:
Quantum theory challenges classical intuitions about state-description, locality, and measurement. It does not present physically actualized contradictions. Experimental outcomes remain determinate enough for physics to function.

---

## 12. Seminar-Ready Summary Paragraph

Any physical theory presupposes that actual states are logically admissible, informationally determinate, and actually instantiated. Logical admissibility constrains what can be physically real. Informational structure provides determinate state-content. Actualization accounts for the obtaining of a manifest physical world rather than a merely formal possibility space. Since Lagrangian and Hamiltonian formalisms already presuppose such a world, they are downstream descriptions of evolution within an already logically actualized dynamical order. The minimal preconditions of physics are therefore not exhausted by physics' own downstream formal machinery.

---

## 13. Tight Closing Formulation

Physics describes how manifested reality behaves. This argument concerns what must be true for there to be any manifested reality for physics to describe.
