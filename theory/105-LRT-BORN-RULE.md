abstract: |
  The Born rule — the prescription that measurement probabilities equal squared amplitudes — is
  derived within the Core Physics reconstruction (100) as a consequence of Gleason's theorem applied
  to the projection-valued measure structure that emerges from binary actualization. This paper
  expands that compressed treatment into a standalone argument addressing three questions: (1) why
  probability enters physics at all given deterministic ontological actualization, (2) why Gleason's
  theorem is the correct bridge from projection structure to probability, and (3) what the Born rule
  means under LRT's ontological interpretation versus epistemic and many-worlds readings.
