# Constitutive informational individuation under the inseparable triad

Status: RESEARCH / ADVERSARIAL MODEL TEST; NOT CANONICAL
Date: 2026-11-09
Work package: WP-LRT-ACT-0001

## Objective

Test whether physically obtaining informational distinctions can individuate actual structures independently of Action profiles, without treating I∞ as a separately obtaining repository. Target the open RA3-1/2/3 obligations. The owner holds that L₃, I∞ and A are inseparable in obtaining; logical priority is not independent or temporal existence.

## Ontological discipline

Distinguish (i) a satisfiable **description** of a configuration, (ii) a coherent possibility described in the triadic framework, (iii) a **physically obtaining** structure, and (iv) its representation. A satisfiable description is not automatically an obtaining possibility, and an obtaining possibility is not automatically a physically actual field. No one primitive is postulated to obtain alone.

**Candidate CI-0:** Two actual structures are ontically different if their physically obtaining, gauge-invariant informational constitutions differ. This is a proposed *sufficient* criterion of difference, not yet a necessary criterion or a derivation of Action relevance.

The phrase “physically obtaining informational constitution” remains a primitive-level semantic obligation: replacing it with “whatever A selects” would reproduce the old Boolean selector abstraction and would not explain co-constitution.

## Imported finite test apparatus

Use a set S of actual structures, an independently specified `Actual(s)`, a set D of descriptions, an interpretation `rep:S→D`, and an equivalence relation `~g` on D representing physically redundant encodings. Define `InfoDiff(x,y)` when `rep(x) !~g rep(y)`. Use an independent ontic equivalence `≡O`, and an Action-profile trace `P:S→Profiles` defined from admissible possible consequences, not from primitive A itself.

- **CI-soundness:** `InfoDiff(x,y) ⇒ x≢O y`.
- **CI-completeness:** `x≢O y ⇒ InfoDiff(x,y)`.
- **RA-3:** `x≢O y ⇒ P(x)≠P(y)`.

CI-soundness and completeness are separate; neither is licensed by the definitions. A quotient D/~g is an imported mathematical tool. The choice of `~g` must be justified independently of Action-profile equality and observational indistinguishability.

## Countermodels

**I1: Numerically distinct isomorphic instances.** Let actual x and y have identical descriptions up to ~g, but `x≢O y` under primitive numerical identity. Then CI-completeness fails. An informational *type* does not individuate multiple *tokens* without location, relations, or a further principle. Counting spatial positions as part of the description risks presupposing spacetime.

**I2: Pure gauge multiplicity.** Let `rep(x)=d₁`, `rep(y)=d₂`, with distinct syntax but `d₁~g d₂` and `x≡O y`. Raw syntactic difference is not physical difference. If ~g is too narrow, CI-soundness can fail; if too broad, CI-completeness can fail.

**I3: Informational difference with identical Action profiles.** Two actual x,y have inequivalent physically meaningful descriptions d₁,d₂ and are ontically distinct, yet `P(x)=P(y)` for all independently admitted contexts. CI-soundness holds while RA-3 fails. Therefore constitutive informational individuation alone does not entail Action separation.

**I4: Action distinction without informational difference.** Two actual x,y have isomorphic intrinsic informational descriptions, but different admissible external interaction contexts and hence different total Action profiles. CI-completeness fails if the external relational context is omitted from `rep`. Adding it requires an independent criterion of which relations belong to the constitution.

**I5: Circular quotient.** Define `d₁~g d₂` exactly when their Action profiles agree. CI-0 then inherits Action-profile individuation and any “proof” of RA-3 is circular. The quotient must be independently fixed.

**I6: Same complete global structure, two labeled objects.** A symmetric obtaining structure has two internally indiscernible nodes related by an automorphism. Distinct labels alone do not establish distinct ontic objects. A structuralist can treat them as relational positions, but whether an automorphism identifies objects or merely descriptions is an additional metaphysical premise.

**I7: Informational constitution with no transition.** A single actual coherent structure has a nontrivial invariant description but no nonidentity licensed transitions. OIP and CI-soundness can hold; intrinsic dynamical fecundity does not follow from informational individuation.

## Conditional results

1. **CI-soundness does not imply RA-3**: I3.
2. **CI-completeness does not follow from informational structuralism alone** without an anti-haecceitist or token-individuation premise: I1/I6.
3. **CI-soundness requires physically justified redundancy equivalence**: I2.
4. **Even CI-soundness + CI-completeness do not entail RA-3**: I3 can be configured so all ontic distinctions are represented in D/~g but P is constant.
5. **Action-profile separation plus CI-completeness** yields `InfoDiff(x,y) ⇒ P(x)≠P(y)` only with CI-soundness; this merely composes independent assumptions and is not a derivation from OIP.

All are finite metamodel implications or counterexamples, not Lean proofs or physical theorems.

## Ontology options

- **H1, constitution-as-obtaining:** an actual structure is its coherent informational constitution obtaining within χ. Needs a noncircular account of obtaining and numerical multiplicity.
- **H2, instantiation:** a physically actual instance realizes a coherent informational specification. Needs an instantiation relation without importing a fourth primitive.
- **H3, relational structuralism:** identity depends on position in the complete obtaining global structure. Needs a criterion of global structure, automorphisms, and whether indistinguishable positions are distinct tokens.

H1 remains parsimonious as a research preference; no test here establishes H1 over H2/H3. None entails RA-3 by itself.

## Proposed independent individuation protocol

1. State the obtaining constitution in non-operational terms; explicitly flag any unexplained actuality predicate.
2. Specify physical redundancy ~g without using Action-profile or record equality.
3. Compare global structures, including relevant relations, modulo ~g and automorphisms.
4. Decide whether numerical multiplicity beyond structural distinction is allowed; preserve both branches until justified.
5. Independently specify admissible Action contexts and profiles.
6. Attempt RA-3; retain I3 as a standing adversarial countermodel.

This protocol is a research methodology, not a derived identity law.

## Outcome and next discriminating obligation

**Result:** Constitutive informational difference can serve as a *candidate sufficient* condition for ontic difference once physically meaningful informational content and gauge equivalence are independently grounded. It cannot, on present premises, establish complete ontic individuation or force differences in possible Action. In particular, I3 blocks the inference from informational distinction to RA-3.

**Next obligation CI-1:** Determine whether co-constitution itself supplies a noncircular criterion for *physically obtaining* information, rather than merely a coherent description, and whether that criterion excludes I3 without equating A to a selector or Action profiles.
