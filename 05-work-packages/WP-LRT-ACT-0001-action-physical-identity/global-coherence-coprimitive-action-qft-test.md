## Owner clarification and central thesis

LRT retains exactly three co-primitives, `χ=[L₃:I∞:A]`. L₃ supplies prescriptive logical constraints, I∞ the informational possibility domain, and A the fundamental ontological requirement for actuality and possible transitions. They are jointly necessary in the intended ontology. Neither L₃ alone nor global coherence alone actualizes a field. No temporal precedence among co-primitives is implied.

**Candidate GCA-0:** An actual quantum field has a globally coherent informational constitution constrained by L₃, while determinate local values need not be assigned to every observable. Particular local interactions and excitations are A-grounded manifestations within the actual structure.

“Global” means compatibility of the full field description across its regions, not an instantaneous action across space, a preferred cosmic time, or a claim that every mathematical global state is physically actual. “Logically real” must be disambiguated: logically admissible possibility is weaker than obtaining physical reality. Coherence does not supply the specific dynamical laws or unique QFT algebra.

## QFT comparison model: algebraic net

Use the *independently imported* AQFT formalism as an adversarial target, not as a derivation from 3FLL. A spacetime region O is associated with a local observable algebra `𝒜(O)`, with isotony `O₁⊆O₂ ⇒ 𝒜(O₁)⊆𝒜(O₂)`. For spacelike-separated regions, microcausality requires commuting local observables. A global state `ω` consistently restricts to each local algebra `ω|𝒜(O)`. This is a compatibility pattern that can represent global coherence alongside regional observability.

This pattern does not prove the converse that any arbitrary family of locally specified states extends to one global state. Nor does it derive microcausality from L₃. Local observables and physically implementable local operations require separate treatment; algebraic commutativity by itself does not resolve all causal measurement protocols.

## Two conditional results

**CR-1 (global restriction compatibility, elementary):** If a single global state `ω` exists on `𝒜` and local algebras embed into it, then for nested regions `O₁⊆O₂`, restrictions agree: `(ω|𝒜(O₂))|𝒜(O₁)=ω|𝒜(O₁)`. Proof: both sides evaluate any `x∈𝒜(O₁)` as `ω(x)`. This is a consequence of the imported state/algebra representation, not a theorem of 3FLL.

**CR-2 (global state need not fix all local outcomes):** For a two-qubit singlet `|ψ⁻⟩=(|01⟩−|10⟩)/√2`, the global pure state has reduced state `ρ_A=I₂/2`. Thus the global state is specified while local spin measurements need not have predetermined outcomes in the standard operational interpretation. This example does not establish the impossibility of every hidden-variable ontology, nor is it a QFT derivation.

## Countermodels and nonimplications

**CM-1: coherence without actuality.** A mathematically consistent, fully specified model of a free scalar field can be considered without the proposition that this model obtains in our world. Logical consistency cannot entail its physical instantiation. Thus `Coherent ⇒ Actual` fails without an actuality premise.

**CM-2: global coherence without local tomography.** Real-Hilbert-space two-rebit models admit globally distinct density matrices `ρ±=(I₄±t(J⊗J))/4` with `J=[[0,-1],[1,0]]` and `0<t≤1`, yet all local product real-symmetric observables yield equal statistics. Hence global coherence plus local product measurements does not entail local tomography. This is a countermodel to a generic inference, not evidence that empirical QFT is real-Hilbert-space quantum theory.

**CM-3: coherent but non-QFT dynamics.** A finite classical stochastic field on a lattice can have globally consistent states, well-defined local restrictions, and transition rules. The 3FLL and coherent global descriptions do not uniquely select complex quantum fields, Lorentz covariance, or Born probabilities.

**CM-4: local non-determinacy does not establish a unique ontology.** The singlet's maximally mixed reduced state supports operational local unpredictability; inferring metaphysical absence of all local beables would require interpretation-specific premises.
