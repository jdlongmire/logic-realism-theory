# Ontic difference and change-as-constitution: G-ACT-1 / G-ACT-2 stress test

Status: RESEARCH / SEMANTIC INDEPENDENCE TEST; NOT CANONICAL
Date: 2026-11-09
Work package: WP-LRT-ACT-0001
Depends on: fundamental-nonidentity-action-test.md; constitutive-action-measurement-boundary.md

## Research target

Owner commitment: A is fundamental, necessarily involves genuine non-identity change, and is indispensable to physical actuality. L₃ and I∞ are inert absent A, but none of the three co-primitives obtains independently. Test whether (i) non-identity can be defined noncircularly, and (ii) change can constitute actuality without being an operation upon already actual states.

**Finding:** An explicitly nonidentity-bearing constitutive relation can be represented consistently in small models without an independently actual prior state or metric time. This is a **relative consistency witness**, not an ontological derivation or empirical explanation. Ontic difference and constitutive dependence remain primitive or independently axiomatized. Logical laws alone do not fix either.

## Type discipline

Let D be descriptions, K be candidate constitutive complexes in a formal semantics, and Q be aspects/positions *within* a complex. A formal candidate is not assumed physically actual. Let `Ob(k)` say k obtains; `Member(q,k)` says q is an internal aspect; `≃ₖ` is the proposed *physical* equivalence relation on aspects, which must be specified independently of mere symbol equality. Let `ACon(k,q,r)` say a constitutive Action relation connects aspects q,r *within* k, without asserting that either obtains independently. Let `Coχ(k)` represent joint triadic constitution, with no proper subset separately obtaining.

Candidate semantics:
- **C-OB:** `Ob(k) ⇒ Coχ(k)`.
- **C-NIC:** `Ob(k) ⇒ ∃q,r [Member(q,k) ∧ Member(r,k) ∧ ACon(k,q,r) ∧ ¬(q≃ₖr)]`.
- **C-LOCAL:** `ACon(k,q,r) ⇒ Member(q,k) ∧ Member(r,k)`.
- **C-EQUIV:** `≃ₖ` is reflexive, symmetric and transitive on internal aspects.
- **C-DESC:** A coherent description in D does not entail Ob(k), even if it denotes k.

**C-NIC is the owner's strong A premise expressed as a conditional axiom.** It is not derivable from C-OB, C-LOCAL or C-EQUIV. `Ob` is still primitive; C-NIC does not give sufficient conditions for obtaining.

## Candidate ontic-difference criteria

**D0 Syntactic inequality:** `q ≠ r`. Inadequate: distinct descriptions may denote one physical state, and gauge transformations can change mathematical representation without physical change.

**D1 Quotient difference:** `[q]_{≃ₖ} ≠ [r]_{≃ₖ}`. Better formally, but only substantive if `≃ₖ` has independent physical justification. Defining equivalence to make ACon nontrivial is circular.

**D2 Invariant discrimination:** There exists an admissible physical invariant or relational property P with `P(q) ≠ P(r)`. A stronger witness when P is grounded without assuming the dynamics being derived. It need not be *locally measurable*, and not every ontic distinction must be observationally accessible. Conversely, choosing P merely as a new label reintroduces D0.

**D3 Counterfactual difference:** q and r have distinct admissible relational/causal consequences. This requires a substantive modal or causal law, so cannot serve as a derivation from 3FLL alone.

**Provisional recommendation:** Use D1 as a mathematical guard and D2 as a substantive research obligation. Do not claim D2 is universally necessary without defending an observability/separation principle.

## Explicit finite models

**M1, consistent internal difference without prior actual relata.** K={k}; Q={u,v}; D={d}; Ob(k)=true; Coχ(k)=true; Member={(u,k),(v,k)}; equivalence is identity on Q; ACon={(k,u,v)}. Neither u nor v belongs to the sort of independently obtaining structures; there is no predicate Ob(u) or Ob(v). C-OB, C-NIC, C-LOCAL and C-EQUIV all hold. No time function exists. **Result:** relative satisfiability of a formal constitutive nonidentity relation. **Limit:** u/v difference and ACon are stipulated; this does not explain how actuality originates or what ontic difference is.

**M2, representational relabeling.** Same as M1 but u≃ₖv, with universal equivalence. C-NIC fails although ACon exists. Thus formal transition between unequal symbols is insufficient for ontic change.

**M3, actual static singleton.** K={k}; Q={u}; Ob(k)=true; Coχ(k)=true; ACon=∅. C-OB, C-LOCAL, C-EQUIV hold; C-NIC fails. Hence the owner's new axiom excludes models formerly admitted by minimal CC-1..5.

**M4, coherent but nonobtaining candidate.** K={k}; Q={u,v}; Ob(k)=false; Coχ(k)=false; ACon=∅; a coherent d∈D denotes k. All conditional axioms hold vacuously. Thus C-NIC does not derive obtaining from coherent possibility.

**M5, arbitrary physical laws with the same change witness.** Extend M1 with a second allowed relation `ACon(k,v,u)` or do not. Both models satisfy C-NIC, and can be assigned incompatible further dynamics. No unique transition law, frequency or probability follows.

**M6, genuine difference with no observed change.** Extend M1 with a coarse-graining observable O(u)=O(v) while independent invariant P(u)≠P(v). This is formally possible; if P is physically ungrounded it is only an invented hidden variable. A stationary effective state does not falsify C-NIC, but does not confirm it.

**M7, circular equivalence.** Define `q≃ₖr` iff no ACon(k,q,r) exists. This need not even be an equivalence relation (symmetry/transitivity can fail), and builds ontic difference out of the Action relation it was supposed to validate. Reject.

## Noncircularity and explanatory assessment

1. **Logical consistency:** M1 establishes that C-OB + C-NIC + C-LOCAL + C-EQUIV are satisfiable in the specified first-order representation. This is a modest conditional result.
2. **No preexisting actual state:** M1 contains only internal relata of one obtaining complex. This avoids a *formal* requirement for two independently obtaining states. It does not prove that an internally asymmetric relation is intelligible without any metaphysical priority, or that all state change can be represented this way.
3. **Nonidentity:** D1 guards against pure relabeling only if physical equivalence is grounded independently. D2 can provide a noncircular witness when an admissible physical invariant is independently specified.
4. **Constitution versus accompaniment:** `Ob(k) ⇒ ACon(...)` expresses necessity, not constitutive explanation. A model where ACon merely accompanies an independently stipulated Ob satisfies the same formula. To express constitutive dependence one needs a further grounding or explanatory relation with independent truth conditions; adding the word “constitutes” is insufficient.
5. **Inertness counterfactual:** Deleting ACon from M1 while holding Ob fixed produces an invalid model under C-NIC. This is axiom-relative dependence, not proof that A is metaphysically productive. A counterfactual `¬A ⇒ ¬Ob` is only informative if removal is well-defined without treating L₃/I∞ as independently existing.
6. **Directionality:** ACon(k,u,v) may be asymmetric, but asymmetry is not established by ontic inequality alone. Direction and physical temporal succession require distinct premises.

## Independence results

- C-OB + C-LOCAL + C-EQUIV ⊬ C-NIC (M3).
- C-OB + C-NIC + C-LOCAL + C-EQUIV ⊬ ∃k Ob(k) (M4).
- C-NIC ⊬ nontrivial observable change (M6).
- C-NIC ⊬ unique dynamics (M5).
- C-NIC ⊬ a noncircular physical criterion of difference (M1 vs M2 and D1 analysis).
- None of these finite structures entails local tomography, complex Hilbert space, or Born probabilities.

## What would advance the theory

**Priority P1, physical equivalence:** Propose a non-question-begging equivalence criterion `≃ₖ` for fundamental informational aspects, including an explicit treatment of symmetry/gauge and how identity persists through change.

**Priority P2, constitutive grounding:** State the semantic distinction between Action *making* k obtain and Action *being present whenever* k obtains. A grounding relation cannot be treated as explanatory solely by naming it.

**Priority P3, direction/order:** Determine whether ontic non-identity plus Action supplies an asymmetric order, or whether that order is an independent ontological commitment.

**Priority P4, empirical constraint:** Specify at least one physically restrictive consequence distinguishing the strengthened Action thesis from an ontology admitting fundamentally static actuality. Do not insulate all putative fundamental change from evidence by definition.

**Priority P5, model verification:** Translate the finite semantics into an executable model checker or Lean *research-only* sandbox when approved; no Lean verification is claimed here.

## Decision

**HIGH (conditional formal):** The owner's A-NIC axiom excludes wholly static actual models and admits a consistent typed representation with internal difference and no prior actual state.  
**HIGH (conditional independence):** The axiom does not entail obtaining, unique dynamics or observable transitions.  
**UNCERTAIN:** A physically grounded, noncircular equivalence criterion and constitutive explanation of actuality.  
**NOT ESTABLISHED:** Derivation of dynamics, measurement, Born rule, or empirical novelty.

This note is a research test, not a canonical reformulation of LRT.
