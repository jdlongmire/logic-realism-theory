# RA-3: Does ontic distinction entail different Action possibilities?

Status: RESEARCH / CONDITIONAL THEOREMS AND COUNTERMODELS; NOT CANONICAL
Date: 2026-11-09
Work package: WP-LRT-ACT-0001

## Scope and question

Under the owner's Ontological Inseparability Principle (OIP), no member of χ=[L₃:I∞:A] obtains independently. This study tests **RA-3 / AI-0**: whether genuinely distinct actual physical structures must differ in their possible Action relations. Fundamental Action remains co-primitive and cannot be identified with the mathematical transition relation used as its provisional trace.

**Core caution:** Any criterion defining ontic identity *as equality of Action profiles* makes RA-3 true by stipulation. This study instead supplies an independent identity predicate and asks whether RA-3 follows.

## Independent identity and profile definitions

Take a two-sorted classical *metamodel*, explicitly imported for discrimination rather than derived from 3FLL:

- Actual structures S and admissible contexts C, with an independently assigned equivalence relation `≡O` for ontic identity. Its intended truth conditions are left as an explicit open metaphysical obligation; it is not defined using Action profiles or observations.
- `Adm(s,c)` denotes admissibility of context c for structure s.
- `Tr(s,c)` is the set of possible ontically typed transitions or interaction consequences. Equal profile comparisons require shared labels, a canonical comparison relation, or an isomorphism-invariant notion of consequence.
- `P(s)` is the total Action-profile signature `{(c,Tr(s,c)) | Adm(s,c)}` after identifying only representational redundancies. This is an **analytical trace**, not primitive A.
- `Actual(s)` and indivisible `Triad` represent OIP with `Actual(s)⇒Triad`. No primitive exists separately in the model.

**RA-3:** `∀x,y [(Actual(x) ∧ Actual(y) ∧ ¬(x≡O y)) ⇒ P(x)≠P(y)]`.

Its contrapositive, under classical logic, is `P(x)=P(y) ⇒ x≡O y` for actual x,y. That is a **separation/completeness** axiom for Action profiles, not a consequence of their definition.

A converse `x≡O y ⇒ P(x)=P(y)` is an independent **profile-invariance** condition required for well-defined physical interpretation. Without invariance, a profile may distinguish mere gauge labels.

## Finite countermodels

**K1: Distinct ontic identity, identical profiles.** Two actual structures x,y, `x≢O y` by an independent primitive identity assignment, one admissible context c, `Tr(x,c)=Tr(y,c)={stay}`. The indivisible triad obtains. OIP, coherence and identity-reflexivity hold; RA-3 fails. This proves OIP does not logically entail RA-3. The ontic distinction is stipulated and its independent metaphysical warrant remains open.

**K2: Gauge duplicate, different syntactic profiles.** Two descriptions x,y with `x≡O y`, but `Tr(x,c)={a}`, `Tr(y,c)={b}` for distinct labels representing the same physical consequence. Raw profile inequality occurs although no ontic distinction exists. A profile must be gauge-invariant before it can individuate reality.

**K3: Distinct profiles without records.** x,y ontically distinct, with `Tr(x,c)={u}`, `Tr(y,c)={v}`, `u≠v` physically; all permitted observation maps erase u/v. RA-3 holds, global operational separation fails. Thus RA-3 does not entail AI-1.

**K4: Global separation without local tomography.** Two composite configurations differ by a globally accessible correlation-sensitive test but all allowed local-product tests have the same outcomes. RA-3 and global operational separation may hold while AI-2 fails. This is a schematic model; the previously documented real two-rebit witness gives a separate concrete quantum example of local-product indistinguishability.

**K5: Different admissible contexts rather than different outcomes.** x,y share identical transition sets wherever both are admissible, but an additional context is admissible only for x. Total profiles differ. Any claim of RA-3 must specify whether context admissibility itself counts as an Action distinction and independently justify admissibility.

**K6: Non-isomorphic but behaviorally equivalent structures.** Two finitely described labeled systems with different internal representation but identical observable/transition behavior under all specified contexts. If internal distinctions are ontic, RA-3 fails; if mere representations, they are identical. The classification depends on a prior ontic criterion, not on behavior alone.

## Conditional results

**T1 (independence):** OIP + ordinary equality/identity logic + coherent descriptions do not imply RA-3, by K1.

**T2 (RA-3 equivalence):** Given actual x,y and a well-defined P, RA-3 is equivalent to `P(x)=P(y) ⇒ x≡O y` (classical contraposition). This does not establish the antecedent as physically valid.

**T3 (two-way identity characterization):** If both RA-3 and profile-invariance hold, `x≡O y ⇔ P(x)=P(y)`. This is a conditional characterization and can collapse into a definition if the premises are not independently warranted.

**T4 (no operational entailment):** RA-3 does not imply global or local record distinguishability, by K3 and K4.

**T5 (gauge obligation):** Without invariance under representational equivalence, `P` is not a well-defined function on ontic equivalence classes, by K2.

## What could supply an independent criterion?

Candidate identity grounds to evaluate rather than assume:
1. **Primitive thisness:** two structures may be ontically distinct despite identical possible Action relations. This blocks RA-3 unless excluded by a separate anti-haecceitist axiom.
2. **Constitutive informational difference:** differences in globally coherent information are ontically distinguishing *if* they obtain; requires a noncircular bridge from admissible information to actual identity. Information may carry gauge redundancy.
3. **Relational structural identity:** two entities differ only if they occupy distinct positions in an obtaining structure; structurally indiscernible nodes challenge whether position suffices.
4. **Action-profile identity:** RA-3 becomes analytic and offers no independent explanation. This is unsuitable as proof.

**Provisional recommendation:** Test option 2, with an independently stated criterion for actual informational distinctions and gauge quotienting. Preserve primitive-thisness and structural indiscernibility as adversarial countermodels. Do not assert RA-3 until the information-to-identity bridge survives these tests.

## Physics and empirical boundary

Neither OIP nor RA-3 alone supplies complex Hilbert space, local tomography, field equations or a Born probability measure. Gauge redundancy illustrates why syntactic distinctions need not be physical; hidden or superselected sectors illustrate why physical distinctions need not be locally measurable. To gain empirical content, an additional independently derived restriction must exclude a physically realizable theory or imply a novel quantitative signature. No such exclusion is established here.

## Open proof obligations

- **RA3-1:** Define ontic identity independently of `P` and operational records.
- **RA3-2:** Specify actual informational distinctions without treating I∞ as an independently obtaining reservoir.
- **RA3-3:** Derive gauge-invariant profiles from co-primitive premises, or label the quotient as imported.
- **RA3-4:** Justify admissible contexts without circularly assuming the distinguishing interaction.
- **RA3-5:** Address primitive-thisness and structurally indiscernible actual entities.
- **RA3-6:** Produce a falsifiable physical consequence beyond a restatement of identity.
