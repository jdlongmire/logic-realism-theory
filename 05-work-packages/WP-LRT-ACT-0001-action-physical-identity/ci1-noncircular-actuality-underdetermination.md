# CI-1: Noncircular actuality and co-constitution

Status: RESEARCH / SEMANTIC NO-GO TEST; NOT CANONICAL
Date: 2026-11-09
Work package: WP-LRT-ACT-0001

## Question and owner commitments

Under χ=[L₃:I∞:A], no co-primitive obtains independently of the others. L₃ has logical priority without temporal or ontological independence; I∞ is not an independently obtaining possibility warehouse; A is co-primitive and cannot be reduced to a Boolean selector, transition operator, or prior event. Can these commitments determine *which* coherent informational constitution physically obtains?

## Distinguish three different questions

1. **Condition for any obtaining:** Why is actuality intelligible only under the inseparable triad?
2. **Determination of a particular obtaining:** Why does constitution x obtain rather than coherent alternative y?
3. **Individuation within obtaining:** When do two descriptions represent one actual entity rather than distinct actual entities?

An answer to (1) does not automatically answer (2) or (3). “Actual means co-constituted” is a candidate constitutive truth condition for (1), but cannot independently explain why a specific x obtains.

## Three candidate semantic approaches

**S1, primitive joint obtaining:** Take `Obtainsχ(x)` as an irreducible fact of the co-constitutive unity. L₃ constrains coherence, I∞ concerns informational articulation, A concerns obtaining and possible transition. `Actual(x) iff Obtainsχ(x)` is a semantic explication, **not a derivation**; it avoids a fourth primitive only if obtaining is genuinely an aspect of χ, rather than a separate ontological agency. Particular selection remains unexplained.

**S2, constitutive constraint:** Require a jointly coherent, informationally determinate and Action-grounded constitution `Cχ(x)`. `Actual(x) ⇒ Cχ(x)` is a necessary-condition claim. The converse `Cχ(x) ⇒ Actual(x)` is an independent sufficiency premise; if all coherent x meet Cχ, it overgenerates actuality. An additional uniqueness or exclusion law must be independently established.

**S3, instantiation relation:** Let a possibility p be instantiated by an actual token t. If p and t are independently obtaining relata, this violates the owner’s inseparability commitment or invites a fourth primitive. An internal representational relation might avoid that but does not explain why t obtains. S3 requires more ontological machinery than S1 unless reduced to co-constitution.

## Explicit finite underdetermination test

Let D={p,q} contain two mutually exclusive but individually coherent complete descriptions. Let all admitted constraints of L₃ and the stipulated joint triad be satisfied in three finite models:

- **U1:** `Obtainsχ(p)`, not `Obtainsχ(q)`.
- **U2:** `Obtainsχ(q)`, not `Obtainsχ(p)`.
- **U3:** neither p nor q obtains (unless a separate existence/sufficiency axiom rules this out).

In U1 and U2, χ is inseparable wherever obtaining occurs; each model respects consistency and a non-Boolean reading of A. They disagree on which description obtains. Thus **OIP and logical coherence do not uniquely select p or q**. U3 tests the distinct question of whether χ necessarily constitutes at least one reality; it is excluded if that existence is separately postulated. If the descriptions are not genuinely mutually exclusive, a fourth model with both obtaining is possible.

The model's `Obtainsχ` predicate is analytical bookkeeping for the *whole triad*, not a selector function attributed to A. No model pretends to reconstruct the internal metaphysics of χ.

## Stronger explanatory challenge: symmetry

Suppose p and q are equally coherent and related by an automorphism of all currently specified constitutive relations. Any proposed selection criterion invariant under that automorphism must treat them alike. It cannot pick exactly one without an additional asymmetry, spontaneous symmetry-breaking law, contingent obtaining fact, or revision of the exclusivity assumption. This is a conditional mathematical obstruction, not a claim that physical actuality has this symmetry.

A principle `ExactlyOneObtains({p,q})` adds exclusivity but still does not determine which member. A deterministic rule that chooses one must expose its premise and avoid disguising it as “A chooses.”

## Modal language discipline

“Possible” may mean (a) a description is logically satisfiable, (b) a constitution is permitted by a proposed physical law, or (c) a genuinely obtaining modal feature of reality. These are distinct. In particular, (a) is a metalinguistic assessment and does not assert an independently existing I∞. Neither (b) nor (c) is derived merely by labeling the domain infinite.

## Counterarguments to anticipated shortcuts

- **Selector shortcut:** `A(x)=actual` names a partition and cannot explain its basis. It remains useful as an explicitly incomplete formal abstraction.
- **Coherence shortcut:** Consistency is necessary but insufficient for a specific physical obtaining; U1/U2 exhibit underdetermination.
- **Identity shortcut:** Defining the actual as “the structure that obtains” states a truth condition but does not explain why it obtains.
- **Dynamics shortcut:** A possible transition relation does not entail an occurring event, and presupposing a time-evolution law imports the sought physics.
- **Observability shortcut:** Operational access is neither a necessary nor a sufficient definition of physical existence.
- **Unique-global-state shortcut:** Uniqueness cannot be inferred from OIP; even if assumed, it must distinguish mathematical uniqueness from physical obtaining.

## Findings

**F1:** The strongest currently defensible actuality claim is **constitutive necessity**: any physically obtaining constitution requires the inseparable χ. The reverse implication and determination of particular actual content remain open.

**F2:** An irreducible *joint obtaining* semantics is compatible with the three-primitives commitment if understood as a semantic aspect of χ, but it offers no noncircular mechanism of selection. Calling it an explanation of particular actuality would overstate the result.

**F3:** Any proposal that all coherent possibilities obtain requires a further argument and faces compatibility/exclusivity questions. Conversely, treating I∞ as independently containing existing alternatives contradicts OIP.

**F4:** CI-1 is **not resolved** by the current premises. The underdetermination is a positive research finding and should constrain subsequent reconstruction claims.
