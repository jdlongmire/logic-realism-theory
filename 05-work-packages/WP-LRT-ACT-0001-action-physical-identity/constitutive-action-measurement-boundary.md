# Constitutive Action versus quantum measurement: research-course correction

Status: RESEARCH / METHODOLOGICAL CORRECTION; NOT CANONICAL
Date: 2026-11-09
Work package: WP-LRT-ACT-0001

## Correction and scope

The preceding CI-1 and joint-constitution notes repeatedly ask why coherent alternative p obtains rather than q. That is a legitimate *optional* metaphysical question, but its priority risks presupposing that co-primitive Action selects among independently existing possibilities. The owner instead proposes χ=[L₃:I∞:A] as an inseparable constitutive unity: no primitive obtains independently, no independent repository of candidate worlds is postulated, and Action is neither a Boolean selector nor an assumed collapse or time-evolution operator.

**Course correction:** investigate what *constitutive Action* means within an actual coherent informational structure before asking whether it chooses one possibility from a set. Preserve the earlier finite selection models as conditional stress tests of any future selection claim, not as a requirement that LRT solve selection at the foundation.

## Three explanatory levels

**C1 Ontological constitution:** what it is for a physically actual informational structure to obtain within χ. Its basic distinction is between an actual structure and a description of a possible structure. This level does not posit a measurement, observer, preferred time, or competing actualized branches.

**C2 Physical dynamics:** what physically possible and occurring changes are, how an actual structure sustains identity through change, and how Action is implicated. Action can be ontologically essential even if a given structure is stationary or has no currently occurring event. Dynamical fecundity remains an independent candidate premise.

**C3 Quantum measurement:** why a quantum description with alternative outcome amplitudes is associated with a definite recorded result, and whether the quantum state is ontic, epistemic, or otherwise. A solution must specify its account of outcomes, evolution, and probabilities; merely saying “A actualizes” is insufficient. The Born rule and local tomography cannot be assumed as foundational premises of an LRT derivation.

C1 is logically upstream in the research program, but that ordering is explanatory, not temporal or a claim that C1 uniquely determines C2 or C3.

## Minimal constitutive semantics (research proposal)

Let `χ-actual(s)` abbreviate “s is an actual informational constitution inseparable from the jointly obtaining triad.” It is a **single primitive-level ontological predication**, not an algorithm computing a selection and not a fourth independently obtaining entity. The predicate is an analytical representation of the intended ontology; its truth conditions remain to be clarified.

- **CC-1 (co-constitution):** `χ-actual(s) ⇒ coherent(s)`, where coherence is a necessary logical constraint. This does not make coherence sufficient.
- **CC-2 (inseparability):** no actual s can be constituted by any proper subset of {L₃,I∞,A}. The subset language is metalinguistic; it does not assign independent existence to members.
- **CC-3 (identity):** any actual s has determinate self-identity, without assuming all distinct actual structures have distinct Action profiles.
- **CC-4 (Action essentiality):** A is constitutively implicated in every actual s. This does not assert a nonidentity transition, an occurring event, or a collapse.
- **CC-5 (description discipline):** `coherent(d)` for a description d does not entail `χ-actual(d)`; descriptions and obtaining structures must be typed separately.

These are candidate ontological explications and necessary conditions, **not** derived theorems of classical logic or an operational definition of A. In particular, CC-4 has no independent dynamical content unless additional justified premises are supplied.

## Separation models and limits

**E1: Stationary actuality.** One coherent actual structure has only identity-preserving possible transitions, or none in the provisional dynamics. CC-1..5 can hold. Therefore constitutive Action does not by itself imply excitation or collapse.

**E2: Two descriptions, one actual structure.** Two inequivalent formal descriptions may represent one obtaining structure after a justified physical equivalence. No fundamental selection event between descriptions is needed. This tests the risk of reifying mathematical alternatives.

**E3: Coherent nonobtaining description.** A description of a consistent but nonobtaining configuration can be entertained without an independently existing possible world. CC-1..5 do not demand that A chooses it or rejects it through a physical event.

**E4: Measurement underdetermination.** Attach to an actual structure an imported quantum state and a measurement protocol with multiple predicted outcomes. CC-1..5 contain no stochastic law, outcome-update map, preferred basis, or observer coupling. They do not determine the recorded outcome or Born probabilities. This is an explicit boundary, not a refutation of C1.

**E5: Different Action theories, same constitution.** Two model extensions agree on CC-1..5 and differ on allowed transitions or outcome rules. Thus C1 does not uniquely fix C2 or C3.

**E6: Constitutive label circularity.** If `χ-actual(s)` is defined only as `A(s)=actual`, the new terminology reproduces the old Boolean partition and provides no account of the triad's co-constitutive unity.

## Research questions to prioritize

1. **CA-1:** Can the phrase “constitutively implicated” be given noncircular truth conditions beyond mere joint mention, without a fourth primitive?
2. **CA-2:** Does the triad entail a meaningful physical distinction between an obtaining informational constitution and a consistent description, or is that distinction currently primitive?
3. **CA-3:** What constitutes physical identity through change without assuming a time parameter or Hilbert space?
4. **CA-4:** Which minimal additional assumptions would connect constitutive Action to physical dynamics, and which are independent?
5. **CA-5:** What evidence or physical restriction could discriminate constitutive A-C from competing ontologies? Without one, acknowledge metaphysical underdetermination.
6. **CA-6:** Only downstream, formulate the quantum measurement problem precisely and test whether a completed LRT dynamics addresses definite records, state evolution and probabilities. No present measurement solution is claimed.

Do not make “why p rather than q?” the default definition of the actuality problem. A may be essential to the *being* of actual structures without being modeled as choosing among a prior inventory.
