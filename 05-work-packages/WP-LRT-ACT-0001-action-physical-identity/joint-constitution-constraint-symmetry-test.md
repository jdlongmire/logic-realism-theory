# Joint-constitution constraints: can χ exclude alternative actualities?

Status: RESEARCH / INDEPENDENCE AND SYMMETRY TEST; NOT CANONICAL
Date: 2026-11-09
Work package: WP-LRT-ACT-0001

## Objective

Continue CI-1 by testing whether inseparable co-constitution χ=[L₃:I∞:A] imposes additional necessary constraints that discriminate which coherent complete constitution obtains. The owner's ontological commitment is that none of the three primitives obtains independently. Do not construe L₃ as a preexisting filter, I∞ as an existing warehouse, or A as a Boolean selector.

## Axiom inventory and classification

**OIP (owner metaphysical premise):** every obtaining reality is co-constituted by inseparable L₃, I∞ and A. It is not a theorem of classical logic.

**NC (logical coherence):** no obtaining constitution includes both a proposition and its negation in the same respect. Formal classical consistency is an imported model representation of the 3FLL, not a full ontology.

**COMP (candidate completeness):** the obtaining constitution resolves every relevant proposition. Completeness is stronger than NC and is *not* a consequence of the law of excluded middle without an additional interpretation of physical determination.

**EX (candidate existence):** at least one complete physical constitution obtains. This is a separate existential commitment, not supplied by a conditional OIP.

**UNIQ (candidate uniqueness):** exactly one complete total constitution obtains. This may be a choice of what counts as a *total* world rather than a physically selective law. It does not choose which world.

**REL (candidate relational closure):** an obtaining constitution includes all relations required for its own physical individuation. The required relations must be independently identified; otherwise this is a schema with no discriminating content.

**DYN (candidate Action closure):** all actually occurring transitions are Action-grounded and respect admissible coherence. This is a necessary constraint on events, not a requirement that events occur or a selection rule for initial constitution.

Only OIP is an explicit owner ontological commitment. NC follows as a stipulated logical constraint under classical representation. COMP, EX, UNIQ, REL and DYN are candidate premises whose ontological warrant remains open.

## Finite test model

Take two mutually exclusive, internally coherent complete descriptions p and q. Let an involution σ exchange p and q and preserve all stipulated L₃ coherence, informational articulation, and Action-licensing relations. Define two models:

- **M_p:** exactly p obtains, with the indivisible triad.
- **M_q:** exactly q obtains, with the indivisible triad.

Both satisfy OIP, NC, COMP, EX and UNIQ. Choose identical closed relations and empty occurrence sets for both; then REL (where meaningful) and DYN are satisfied. σ maps M_p to M_q while preserving all preselection premises.

**Result J1:** OIP+NC+COMP+EX+UNIQ+REL+DYN, under these non-discriminating interpretations, does not determine whether p or q obtains. Both are models. If REL or DYN is strengthened with asymmetric content, that content must be independently stated and justified.

**Result J2:** EX excludes the empty actuality model, but does not choose p over q.

**Result J3:** UNIQ excludes simultaneous obtaining of incompatible complete worlds, but does not select the unique world. If the totality is defined as the aggregate of everything obtaining, uniqueness can become definitional.

**Result J4:** NC excludes contradictions; it does not entail a particular actual truth assignment. Classical excluded middle gives p∨¬p, not Actual(p)∨Actual(¬p) without a bridge from truth to physical obtaining.

## Symmetry obstruction (conditional)

If all premises and the selection rule are invariant under σ, no **uniquely determined** selected member of {p,q} can be derived while preserving σ. A rule selecting p but not q requires an asymmetric premise, a non-invariant boundary condition, a symmetry-breaking mechanism, or a primitive contingent fact. Symmetry breaking by itself still requires an account of which branch obtains.

This is a metatheoretic statement about specified models, not a theorem that actual reality must have such a symmetry.

## Adversarial cases

1. **Consistent but unactualized description:** NC holds, but no physical obtaining follows.
2. **Two coherent candidate worlds:** EX+UNIQ ensure one obtains without determining which.
3. **Stationary complete constitution:** DYN holds vacuously if no event occurs; Action remains co-primitive.
4. **Gauge-redundant candidates:** if p and q are two representations of one constitution, σ is representational; the apparent choice disappears. An independently grounded gauge criterion is needed.
5. **Different physical candidates with identical Action profiles:** informational difference alone cannot supply an Action-based selection rule.
6. **Asymmetric constitutive constraint:** a new premise `K(p)` and `¬K(q)` can exclude q, but the explanatory burden moves to why K is entailed by χ rather than imported.

## What could genuinely advance the derivation?

A candidate principle must (a) be independently motivated by the *meaning* of co-constitution, (b) be stated without using the target obtaining fact in its antecedent, (c) exclude at least one explicit countermodel, (d) survive relabeling/gauge equivalence, and (e) avoid importing a preferred time, Hilbert structure, Born probabilities or QFT dynamics.

**Recommended test:** investigate a constrained *mutual-dependence* semantics: each of the three aspects is necessary for a single obtaining constitution, with no explanatory regress or separately existing relata. Prove whether this strengthens OIP beyond necessary co-presence; then rerun M_p/M_q. If both remain models, acknowledge that constitutive explanation of actuality-as-such is the current reach and particular actuality is contingent or still unexplained.

## Explicit proof obligations

- **JC-1:** Specify a non-propositional semantics for inseparability without three independent objects.
- **JC-2:** Distinguish explanatory dependence from logical entailment and physical causation.
- **JC-3:** Justify or reject EX, COMP, UNIQ, REL and DYN individually.
- **JC-4:** Identify any independent asymmetric constraint that discriminates p and q.
- **JC-5:** Determine whether the sought explanation is of existence, specific content, or both.
- **JC-6:** If a discriminating principle is found, connect it to falsifiable physical consequences before elevating its epistemic status.

## Conclusion

**HIGH (conditional model-theoretic result):** the specified symmetric premises do not discriminate between M_p and M_q. **UNCERTAIN (metaphysics):** whether a stronger, independently justified constitutive dependence principle can break that symmetry. **NOT ESTABLISHED:** an actuality selection theorem, physical dynamics, quantum reconstruction, or empirical prediction.
