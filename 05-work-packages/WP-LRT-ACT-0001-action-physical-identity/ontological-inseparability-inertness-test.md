# Ontological Inseparability Principle: dynamical inertness discrimination test

Status: RESEARCH / CONDITIONAL ARGUMENT AND COUNTERMODELS; NOT CANONICAL
Date: 2026-11-08
Work package: WP-LRT-ACT-0001

## Owner decision

The three co-primitives `χ=[L₃:I∞:A]` **cannot obtain independently of one another**. This is stronger than the claim that three independently existing ingredients happen to interact. Their roles are conceptually distinguishable but their obtaining is co-constitutive. The 3FLL retain explanatory/logical priority without independent existence or temporal precedence. A is an ontological requirement, not a mere Boolean selector or an operator of QFT.

**OIP (candidate formulation):** For any obtaining reality, logical constraint, informational possibility, and fundamental Action are inseparable constitutive conditions; no one primitive obtains in isolation. The three are neither temporal stages nor separately existing substances.

## Formal caution: necessity, inseparability and sufficiency

In a propositional *metamodel* with predicates `Obtains(L)`, `Obtains(I)`, `Obtains(A)`, OIP entails their equivalence in obtaining: `Obtains(L) ↔ Obtains(I) ↔ Obtains(A)`. This is only an extensional shadow of inseparability, **not** a definition or adequate reduction of the primitives. If `R` denotes physically obtaining reality, the owner commitment is `R ⇒ (L∧I∧A)` plus inseparability. `(L∧I∧A) ⇒ R` is a distinct sufficiency thesis. These propositional symbols are analytical bookkeeping; interpreting the primitives as separate Boolean substances would contradict OIP.

An additional ambiguity remains: whether a statement that a possibility is logically coherent means that it *obtains as a possibility* or merely that a description is satisfiable in a hypothetical model. Only the latter can be assessed without presupposing the obtaining triad.

## Main question

Does OIP exclude a physically actual but dynamically inert information structure? Distinguish:

- **No currently occurring transition:** a field exists, with no event in a specified interval or context.
- **No possible nontrivial transition:** no state distinct from its present state is A-licensed.
- **No Action at all:** ontological A is absent.
- **Mathematical stationarity:** a state is invariant under an imported time-evolution operator.

These are different claims. In particular, a stationary state can have nontrivial physical correlations and interactions with other systems; no change in a chosen representation does not demonstrate absence of Action.

## Separating models

**M1: Inseparable triad with no occurring transitions.** Model a constituted field `F` with one presently obtaining configuration `s`, an Action-licensed alternative `t`, and `Licensed(s,t)=true` but `Occurs(s,t)=false`. The triad obtains together throughout; no event is required at the current stage. This shows OIP plus transition *capacity* does not entail continuous excitation. The choice of a time interval is not essential to the logical example.

**M2: Inseparable triad with no nontrivial transition available.** Let the constituted structure have one admissible configuration `s`, with only identity-preserving transitions. Assume A is constitutive of its obtaining, but do not assume A necessarily licenses `s→t` for `t≠s`. OIP is satisfied by stipulation, yet the structure is dynamically inert. Thus **OIP alone does not logically rule out absolute dynamical inertness**. Whether M2 is metaphysically admissible is an independent question about the essential meaning of Action.

**M3: Actuality without A.** Stipulate `L` and `I` obtain and a physical field is actual while `A` does not obtain. This is a countermodel to a weaker account in which coherence suffices for actuality; it violates OIP by construction, so it is not an OIP model.

**M4: Action capacity without global observability.** Two distinct admissible configurations may have distinct Action-licensed continuations while all specified local records agree. OIP does not imply local tomography or global operational separation; the observational bridge requires additional premises.

## Conditional theorem and proof

**T1 (inertness exclusion under dynamical fecundity):** Suppose (i) OIP, (ii) each actual structure has at least one admissible state, and (iii) **DF**: for every actual structure F and admissible state s, there exists a distinct admissible state t such that `Licensed_F(s,t)`. Then no actual structure is dynamically inert in the sense of lacking possible nonidentity transitions.

Proof: Fix F and s. DF provides t≠s with `Licensed_F(s,t)`; therefore the absence of every nontrivial licensed transition is false. QED. OIP is not used in the inference except to characterize the ontological setting; the substantive work is done by DF. This is a **conditional trivial consequence**, not a derivation of DF or quantum dynamics from OIP.

**T2 (no event entailment):** Even OIP+DF does not entail that any licensed transition occurs, absent a licensing-to-occurrence principle. M1 supplies a counterexample.

## Candidate alternatives requiring owner decision

- **OIP-minimal:** Action is necessary for actuality, without stipulating nontrivial change for every actual structure. Compatible with M2.
- **OIP-capacity:** Action is necessary for actuality and every actual structure has some genuine nonidentity transition possibility. Excludes M2 by an independent DF axiom.
- **OIP-event:** Action requires at least one actual transition. Stronger and risks regress or a hidden time premise; no derivation supplied.

**Recommendation:** Retain OIP-minimal provisionally, with the Action transition-capacity thesis treated as a distinct research conjecture rather than a smuggled definition. Investigate whether DF is defensible and whether “no change” can be physically meaningful without presupposing time. No owner approval to promote DF as canonical is inferred.

## QFT boundary check

A free-field vacuum or stationary energy eigenstate is not equivalent to an absence of Action. Stationarity concerns a chosen Hamiltonian and evolution representation; vacuum correlations, accessible excitations, and possible interactions remain. Conversely, a mathematical one-state toy model is a consistency countermodel, not a physically realistic QFT model. Quantum mechanics does not directly adjudicate whether ontological A is present because A is an LRT primitive with no operational criterion yet established.

## Discriminating obligations

1. **OIP-1:** Give an ontological semantics of co-constitution that does not presuppose three independently obtaining objects.
2. **OIP-2:** Distinguish an *obtaining possibility* from a merely satisfiable description without an independent I∞ reservoir.
3. **OIP-3:** Determine whether A essentially entails nontrivial transition capacity; justify DF rather than define it into existence.
4. **OIP-4:** Explain how an actual event can obtain without treating A as a prior event or invoking an infinite causal regress.
5. **OIP-5:** Derive any empirically discriminating consequence before asserting QFT reconstruction or a falsifiable prediction.
6. **OIP-6:** Reconcile current canonical selector notation `A(i)=actual` with the co-primitive ontology through a separately approved semantic revision.

## Outcome

**Result:** OIP alone excludes an actuality model missing A, but does not exclude an actual structure without nontrivial possible transitions. Excluding dynamical inertness requires a stronger premise about A. Continuous actual excitation is not entailed even by a universal transition-capacity premise. This is a precise limitation, not a failure of OIP.
