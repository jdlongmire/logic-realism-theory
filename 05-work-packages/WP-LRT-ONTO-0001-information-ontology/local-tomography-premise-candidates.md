# Local tomography: non-circular premise candidates and decision test

Status: RESEARCH / NO CANONICAL AXIOM ADDED
Date: 2026-11-08
Baseline: PR #104's real-quantum two-system countermodel, merged at `c7872a515d1d82fe6a4e7ce8832f4d6b583ee186`.
Owner question: Can an independently motivated LRT principle entail local tomography without restating the conclusion?

## Formal target

Let `S_AB` be composite states, `E_A,E_B` local allowed effects, and `T_AB:S_AB→[0,1]^(E_A×E_B)` the map `T_AB(s)(e,f)=(e⊗f)(s)`. Local tomography is exactly the injectivity of `T_AB`. Merely postulating injectivity (LPES) is circular as an explanation of it.

Distinguish four properties:
- (D) distinct configurations have determinate identity;
- (G) distinct states are separated by at least one **global** allowed effect;
- (L) distinct states are separated by some **local product** effect;
- (C) local measurements can be coordinated and their joint outcome probabilities compose according to a specified product rule.

The known 2-rebit witness satisfies D and G but violates L. A bare C, without an effect-spanning/completeness condition, need not yield L.

## Three candidate routes

### Candidate A: local relational completeness

**Proposal:** All composite identity-bearing degrees of freedom are generated by locally implementable effect correlations, with no independent global sectors.

**Benefit:** Direct connection to LRT's relational account of identity.

**Defect:** Unless 'generated' has a separately motivated operational definition and an independent proof, this restates injectivity of `T_AB` and therefore begs the question. The real-quantum `J⊗J` degree of freedom is the explicit test case.

**Disposition:** NOT ACCEPTABLE as a derivation in its present form.

### Candidate B: compositional process tomography

**Proposal:** A physically possible global transformation or state preparation is distinguishable by finite protocols assembled from local instruments, local classical communication, and permitted ancillary systems.

**Benefit:** Operationally testable and potentially grounded in a richer theory of admissible interactions.

**Defect:** LOCC with ancillas is not automatically identical to one-shot product-effect local tomography; process accessibility may be stronger or weaker depending on allowed ancillary resources and composition rules. Must formally define allowed instruments and show a reduction to injectivity of `T_AB`.

**Falsification test:** Construct a GPT with the proposed protocol-completeness property but a nontrivial kernel of `T_AB`; if one exists, candidate B fails as sufficient.

**Disposition:** INVESTIGATE; currently an added physical-operational premise.

### Candidate C: universal information-unit / reversible interaction structure

**Proposal:** Derive a sufficiently restrictive composite state space from a capacity-two information unit, continuous reversible dynamics, interaction richness, and a compositional axiom, then prove product effects separate composite states.

**Benefit:** Connects to existing GPT reconstruction research and could replace an unexplained local-tomography postulate with different substantive premises.

**Defect:** Most cited reconstructions already assume local tomography explicitly. A theorem that uses it cannot be used to derive it. In particular, the 2011 Masanes–Müller R2 is local tomography, and the 2012/2016 authors' four-postulate exposition retains it.

**Falsification test:** Locate a precise theorem without tomography among its premises; exhibit its derivation and map every remaining premise to LRT, without adding an unacknowledged local-effect separation assumption.

**Disposition:** INVESTIGATE ONLY WITH PRIMARY THEOREM CHECK.

## Algebraic obstruction: what must be excluded

For two real two-level systems let `J=[[0,-1],[1,0]]` and `K=J⊗J`. The composite states `ρ±=(I₄±tK)/4`, `0<t≤1`, have the same statistics for every product of real symmetric local effects. The global effect `(I₄+K)/2` distinguishes them by `t`.

Thus any proposed theorem from D, G, or an LRT ontological bridge to L must specify **which hypothesis excludes K**. Without that exclusion, the purported derivation is invalid.

Independent numerical check in Python/NumPy at `t=0.6`:
- `eigvalsh(ρ+) = eigvalsh(ρ-) = [0.1,0.1,0.4,0.4]`;
- `Tr[(ρ+−ρ−)(I₄+K)/2] = 0.6`;
- example local product effect `diag(1,0)⊗I₂/2` gives separation `0`.

This checks one product effect numerically; the universal product-effect claim follows from the analytic identity `Tr(JE)=0` for every real symmetric E.

## Recommended theory decision

1. Retain LRT's three primitives and the corrected `C/AΩ` distinction.
2. Keep `Determinate Identity→Local Tomography` marked **REQUIRES_NEW_PREMISE** in the entailment audit.
3. Do not promote any candidate A–C to an axiom. Require a non-circular theorem statement, a countermodel search, and a premise-by-premise derivation.
4. In the next canonical prose revision, distinguish 'global operational determinacy' from 'local product-effect completeness' and label the latter an unresolved obligation.
5. Keep Muse's Step0/Step1 Lean work separate; this document changes no formalization code.
