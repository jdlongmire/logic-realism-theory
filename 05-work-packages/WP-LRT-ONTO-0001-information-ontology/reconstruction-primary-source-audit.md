## Finding relevant to LRT core physics §3.2

The core manuscript currently attributes a four-condition shorthand to the 2011 Masanes–Müller theorem: local tomography, continuous reversible dynamics, existence of entangled states, and unrestricted observables. The *2011 primary abstract* says five physical requirements; the *2012 authors' summary* enumerates four but includes equivalence of systems of equal information capacity, which the manuscript's shorthand omits. Thus the four-condition list must not be represented as a verified exhaustive theorem hypothesis list for 2011.

The correct provisional epistemic status is **CONDITIONAL IMPORT, PRECISE HYPOTHESES PENDING FULL-TEXT AUDIT**. Until primary theorem statements and definitions are compared, no conclusion follows that LRT's local tomography plus its listed three inputs uniquely selects complex Hilbert space.
