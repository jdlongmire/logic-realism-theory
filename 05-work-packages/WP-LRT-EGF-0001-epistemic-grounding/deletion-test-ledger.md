# Deletion-Test Ledger

Status: OPEN
Work package: WP-LRT-EGF-0001

## Method

For each candidate constitutive condition Y, test whether removing Y makes the target relation merely false or unsuccessful, or makes it non-instantiable as the kind of relation under examination.

Disposition values:

- SURVIVES
- DERIVATIVE
- REQUIRES_QUALIFICATION
- REDUNDANT
- FAILS

## Evidence candidates

| Candidate | Target | Deletion question | Initial status |
|---|---|---|---|
| identity | Evidence(E,H) | Can E or H be determinate relata without identity? | UNTESTED |
| differentiation | Evidence(E,H) | Can evidential bearing exist without distinguishable relata? | UNTESTED |
| determinacy | Evidence(E,H) | Can E bear on H if either is wholly indeterminate? | UNTESTED |
| information | Evidence(E,H) | Does evidential constraint require informational content, or is information derivative? | UNTESTED |

| inferential constraint | Evidence(E,H) | Can something bear epistemically on H absent any constraint on permissible conclusions? | UNTESTED |

## Dependency-reduction questions

1. Does identity entail sufficient differentiation for the framework?
2. Is determinacy primitive, or does it arise from identity plus differentiation?
3. Is information a constitutive primitive of evidence, or a description of determinate difference?
4. Is relation primitive, or already contained in BearsOn and consequence?
5. Does truth-aptness apply to H only, to both E and H, or only to propositional evidence?
6. Does inferential constraint belong inside the definition of evidence or only in the transition from evidence to conclusion?
